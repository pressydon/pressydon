# GreenNaijaEnergy — Energy Management & Green Energy

**Project type:** Product initiative  
**Public website:** https://greennaijaenergy.com  
**Portfolio owner:** Precious Dim

## Overview

GreenNaijaEnergy is an energy-management initiative focused on helping Nigerians manage power use and explore greener energy options, with the potential to expand into other markets.

## Product problem

Energy decisions can be difficult when households and businesses lack clear ways to understand their usage, compare options, and assess the value of cleaner alternatives.

## My contribution

I developed the web application and helped shape the product direction around energy management and green-energy adoption.

## Technology

React, TypeScript, Zustand, and NestJS.

## Engineering focus

- Building a user-facing web product around a practical local problem
- Organizing frontend state and application workflows
- Designing for future product growth and potential market expansion
