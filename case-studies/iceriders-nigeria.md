# Iceriders Nigeria — Multi-Sided Logistics & Delivery Platform

**Project type:** Logistics and delivery platform  
**Public website:** https://iceridersnigeria.com  
**Portfolio owner:** Precious Dim

## Overview

Iceriders is a logistics and delivery platform serving multiple user groups, including customers and delivery agents, with supporting web and administrative workflows.

## My contribution

I contributed to the development and launch of platform experiences spanning customer-facing applications, delivery-agent mobile applications, web interfaces, and administration.

## Technology areas

React, React Native, and API/backend integrations.

## Engineering focus

- Supporting different user journeys across web and mobile
- Building workflows for customers and delivery agents
- Connecting application experiences with operational administration
- Working across a multi-sided product ecosystem
